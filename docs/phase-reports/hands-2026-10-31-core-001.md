### Important implementation boundaries

- **[TESTED-ONLY]** Exact-destination synthetic adapter subclasses Executor only
  to replace its implicit `_to_conflicts` rewrite with refusal. Its move/trash,
  serialization, write-ahead journal, hashes, undo and locks remain the real
  library. `resolve_collision` is an additive tool spelling for an **explicitly
  hashed** new path, not permission to overwrite the old target. Late races
  refuse instead of silently choosing a new destination after approval.
- **[TESTED-ONLY]** Generic non-T3 binary requests are not restricted to the
  observe allowlist: they can reach the fake runner under exact T2 approval and
  an independent re-read oracle. Unimplemented tool adapters and missing
  verification do not masquerade as completed work. This is the general loop
  with a synthetic adapter set, not a claim that every future tool already ships.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The command analyser and lexical detector are not containment
  guarantees. This increment has no real task spawner at all; any future broker
  must supply real executable identity/effect sensors and direct argv execution.
- **[TESTED-ONLY]** R4 privacy permits metadata only. The two fixed R3 diagnostics
  (`reason=unadjudicable`, `two-mismatch-halt`) are the explicitly ruled exception,
  not an arbitrary reason-text channel. No owner intent, task output or exact
  T2 command is added to journal detail.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The standalone QML window remains a mock presentation, not
  a service client. Backend queue and JS grammar are connected in tests, not
  through a shipped GUI IPC bridge. No screenshot or mock click is accepted as
  an owner approval or hardware scene result.
