# SPEC — Provider routing: one voice, many hands (owner ruling)

**Owner ruling (2026-11-25):** Gemini is not the only free API.
Her *personality* stays on ONE provider; everything else may be
diverted. And the budget is **FREE** — free tiers and local models
only; no paid keys, no card, ever. If a capability cannot be had
free or local, it is deferred, not purchased.

## Why personality is pinned (owner's reasoning, adopted)

> "I still want to limit her personality to one api bc the way
> ChatGPT will carry it out isn't the same way Claude will."

Correct, and structural: a persona is not fully portable. The
prompt, `humour.toml`, and memory travel — but each model family
*carries* a persona differently (tone, timing, when it jokes, how
it pushes back). If her conversational self were load-balanced
across providers, she would feel like several similar people, and
the drift would be unmeasurable. Therefore:

- **PERSONALITY PIN:** every response that *is her speaking* —
  spoken answers, chat replies, narration, teasing, pushback —
  is generated by the pinned personality provider (**Gemini**,
  the flash-lite workhorse tier; flash only on escalation) and
  voiced by her local TTS. No other provider ever generates
  owner-facing personality text.
- Other providers may produce **raw material** (summaries,
  extractions, classifications). When she speaks from that
  material, the *speaking* still happens on the pinned provider —
  she re-voices it in her own words. A summary from a foreign
  model is data (IF-6), never her voice.
- If the pinned provider is down/limited: she gets *quieter*
  (shorter, local-only, "I'm on budget"), never *someone else*.
  There is no failover of personality. This is a refusal posture,
  not an outage.

## The routing table (design target)

| Voice (TTS) | local Piper | free forever |

| Bulk classification, extraction, dedupe | diverted free provider or local | free tier |

## Hard boundaries (non-negotiable)

1. **One account per provider, the owner's own.** No multiple
   accounts anywhere to stack quotas; no other people's keys.
   (The 4-friends key pool is dead — ToS circumvention, recorded.)
2. Each provider is used inside its own terms. Occasional
   failover when a provider rate-limits is resilience; systematic
   rotation designed to multiply free quota is evasion — refused.
3. **$0.** A provider whose useful tier requires a card on file
   is treated as paid and rejected for routine routing.
4. Fetched/diverted content is untrusted data (IF-6) wherever it
   was produced.
5. Every provider call is cost-ledger journaled (provider, model,
   tokens, purpose) so "what did today cost, per provider" is
   always answerable. Budget declarations per the procedure.

## Degradation ladder (what "down due to limit" must look like)

1. Gemini flash exhausted → flash-lite only (already true).
2. flash-lite exhausted → diverted providers handle *material*;
   her spoken replies shorten and she says she's on budget.
3. All cloud gone → local-only: wake, lock, transcription,
   memory, reminders still work. She is quieter, never dead.
4. Recovery is automatic at quota reset; no state is lost,
   because memory and identity never lived in any provider.

  50 req/day `:free` remains a possible second source later).
  Local Ollama rejected by the owner: the laptop hardware
  isn't up to it. Cloudflare stays a built-but-unenabled
  adapter.
- **Scope: ALL material jobs divert** — errand `content.analyze`
  (organise), REACH summarisation, classification/extraction/
  dedupe. Personality is untouched: Gemini pinned in code,
  re-voicing of diverted material on Gemini, no personality

- **Degradation: diverted provider down/limited → fall back to
  Gemini flash-lite** for material (still re-voiced; still not
  a personality failover — her voice never moved). Local-only
  remains the floor when all cloud is gone, per the ladder.
- Enablement is the owner's deliberate act per BUILD-9R/9S:
  own Groq account + `ELARIA_GROQ_API_KEY` in the daemon's
